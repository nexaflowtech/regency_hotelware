Accounting Module

In ERPNext, accounting isn't a separate manual step — every Purchase, Stock and Sales document posts its own General Ledger entry automatically. Consolidated below for visibility.

ERPNext · Accounts
Triggered from Purchase

Stock Received But Not Billed

Posted automatically the moment a Purchase Receipt is submitted, before the vendor invoice arrives.

Purchase Receipt → GL Entry
Triggered from Purchase

Accounts Payable Entry

Creditor liability booked when the Purchase Invoice is submitted against the vendor.

Purchase Invoice → GL Entry
Triggered from Purchase

Debit Note / Return Reversal

Reverses stock and payable value when rejected material is returned to the vendor.

Purchase Invoice Return → GL Entry
Triggered from Manufacturing

Job Card Cost Posting

Operation cost for Blank, Bending, Welding and Assembly is booked to Work-in-Progress automatically on each Job Card.

Job Card → GL Entry
Triggered from Manufacturing

WIP to Finished Goods Transfer

Value moves from the Work-in-Progress account to the Finished Goods account on Stock Entry (Manufacture).

Stock Entry → GL Entry
Triggered from Sale

Accounts Receivable Entry

Debtor value booked the moment the Sales Invoice is submitted against the customer.

Sales Invoice → GL Entry
Triggered from Sale

Cost of Goods Sold

COGS posted automatically from the Finished Goods account when the Delivery Note is submitted.

Delivery Note → GL Entry
Triggered from Purchase & Sale

Bank / Cash Settlement

Every vendor payment and customer receipt clears against the Payable or Receivable ledger.

Payment Entry → GL Entry

Note: all GL postings above are system-generated by ERPNext at the moment the linked document is submitted — no separate manual accounting step is required.
